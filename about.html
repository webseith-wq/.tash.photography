About TASH Photography

Your Vision, Our Creation

Based in Kampala, Uganda, TASH Photography is dedicated to capturing your most precious moments with precision and creativity. Whether it is a wedding, a graduation, or a corporate branding shoot, we bring your vision to life.

Beyond photography, we offer a wide range of services including professional printing, computer training, custom t-shirt labelling, motorcycle stickers, and passport photos. We are your one-stop shop for all things media and branding.
